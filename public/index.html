      How to play

      You and an AI cat compete in a semantic tug-of-war. Each round, you each submit one word. The closer your word is in meaning to your target (and the further from your opponent's), the harder you pull the rope to your side.

      Scoring

      For every word, the server computes similarity(word, your_target) − similarity(word, opponent_target). Positive scores pull the rope toward you; negative scores pull toward the AI.

      Sudden Death

      After round 5, the pit at the center starts to widen each round and the win threshold shrinks fast. Most games are decided by round 7–10 — don't let your cat fall in!

      Rules

      • Real English words only.
      • No morphological duplicates — if "ocean" has been played, you can't play "oceans" or "oceanic".
      • Two cats. One pit. May the better word win.

      2-player mode

      Switch to 2 players (local) in Settings to play head-to-head on the same screen — pass the keyboard each turn. P1 controls the black cat, P2 controls the orange one.
